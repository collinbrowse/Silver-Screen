# Epic: TV Episode

The detail screen for one episode, opened from a season.

| # | Story | Done |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | When you click on an episode from the list of episodes in a TV Season, you are directed here. The UI contains the main image, episode title, episode number, overview, and air date. Under that, a More Episodes carousel shows the other episodes in the same season; tapping one opens that episode. | [x] |
| 2 | Underneath that, a carousel of images for the episode. Clicking on an image makes it full screen (and can be dismissed). | [x] |
| 3 | Underneath the above, a carousel of actors in the episode. Limit to 30 people. UI includes a picture, the character name, and the actor's real name. Clicking a cell takes you to that person. | [x] |
| 4 | Underneath the above, a carousel of guest stars in the episode. Limit to 30 people. UI includes a picture, the character name, and the actor's real name. Clicking a cell takes you to that person. | [x] |
| 5 | Underneath the above, a carousel of the episode's directors and writers (jobs Director, Writer, and Screenplay). Limit to 30 people. UI includes a picture, the job, and the person's name. Clicking a cell takes you to that person. | [x] |
