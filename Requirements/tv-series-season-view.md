# Epic: TV Series Season View

The detail screen for one season of a series.

| # | Story | Done |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | If you click a TV series season cell from the TV Series View, you are pushed to this view. You see a poster, TV series name, the heading `{number} · {name}`, overview, and air date. | [x] |
| 2 | Underneath that, a carousel of images for the season. Clicking on an image makes it full screen (and can be dismissed). | [x] |
| 3 | Underneath the above, a carousel of actors in the season. Sort by episode count (highest first). Limit to 30 people. UI includes a picture, the character name, and the actor's real name. Clicking a cell takes you to that person. | [x] |
| 4 | Underneath the above, a carousel of directors and writers of the season. Sort by episode count (highest first). Limit to 30 people. UI includes a picture, the character name, and the actor's real name. Clicking a cell takes you to that person. | [x] |
| 5 | Underneath that, a list of all episodes in that season. Each cell contains: the main image, episode title, episode number, overview, air date, and the director. | [x] |
