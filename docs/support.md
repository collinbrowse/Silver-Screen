# Help & Support

- **On GitHub:** [open an issue](https://github.com/collinbrowse/Silver-Screen/issues/new).

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. There's no sign-up, and everything you add is saved on your device.

**How do I keep my library when I get a new phone?**
Your library is saved with the rest of your iPhone or iPad backup. Set up the new device from an iCloud or computer backup, and your lists, ratings, notes, and watch history come with it.

**If I delete the app, is my library gone?**
Yes. Deleting the app also deletes your library from that device. You can get it back by restoring a backup made before you deleted the app.

**Where does the movie and TV information come from?**
Titles, cast, and artwork come from [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/). Where to stream comes from [JustWatch](https://www.justwatch.com/), through TMDB. Awards history comes from [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/). TMDB is a community-edited database, so if a title's details are wrong, anyone with a free TMDB account can fix them there, and the app picks up the change.

**A title or award is missing. What can I do?**
Send feedback from the app and name the title or award. The developer will look into it and add it if possible.
