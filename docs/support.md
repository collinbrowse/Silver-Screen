# Help & Support

## Contact

- **In the app:** Library → Settings (gear) → Send Feedback. Add your email if you'd like a reply.
- **On GitHub:** [open an issue](https://github.com/collinbrowse/Silver-Screen/issues/new).

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. Everything you add stays on your device.

**How do I keep my library when I get a new phone?**
Your library is part of your iPhone or iPad backup. Restore the new device from an iCloud or computer backup and your lists, ratings, and notes come with it.

**If I delete the app, is my library gone?**
Yes. Deleting the app removes its data from the device. A backup made before you deleted it still has it.

**Where does the movie and TV information come from?**
[TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/). Streaming availability comes from [JustWatch](https://www.justwatch.com/) through TMDB, and awards history from [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/). If a title's details are wrong, they can be corrected on TMDB.

**A title or award is missing.**
Send feedback from the app with the title, and it will be looked into.
