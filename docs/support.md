# Help & Support

## Contact

- **Email:** [silverscreen.camera905@slmails.com](mailto:silverscreen.camera905@slmails.com)
- **In the app:** go to Library, tap the gear to open Settings, then tap Send Feedback. Add your email if you'd like a reply.
- **On GitHub:** [open an issue](https://github.com/collinbrowse/Silver-Screen/issues/new).

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. There's nothing to sign up for, and everything you add stays on your device.

**How do I keep my library when I get a new phone?**
Your library is saved in your iPhone or iPad backup. Set up the new device from an iCloud or computer backup, and your lists, ratings, and notes come with it.

**If I delete the app, is my library gone?**
Yes. Deleting the app removes your library from the device. If you made a backup before deleting it, restoring that backup brings your library back.

**Where does the movie and TV information come from?**
Titles, cast, and details come from [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/). Where to stream comes from [JustWatch](https://www.justwatch.com/), through TMDB. Awards history comes from [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/). If a title's details are wrong, anyone can fix them on TMDB, and Silver Screen picks up the correction.

**A title or award is missing. What should I do?**
Send feedback from the app with the name of the title or award, and the developer will look into it.
