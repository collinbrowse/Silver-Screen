# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

Silver Screen is built so your library stays yours. There is no account to create, no advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites. Nothing about you is sold or shared for marketing.

## What stays on your device

Your lists, ratings, notes, and TV progress are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. Like any other app data, they are included when you back up your device to iCloud or a computer. The developer never receives them. Deleting the app deletes them.

## What leaves your device, and why

**Movie and TV information.** To show titles, people, search results, and streaming availability, the app requests them from [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/) (The Movie Database). A request includes what you searched for or opened, but nothing from your library. Like any website, TMDB can see your IP address when the app connects to it. See [TMDB's privacy policy](https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy).

**Trailers.** Trailers play from YouTube. When you play one, [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies to that video.

**Awards catalog.** The app checks GitHub for an updated list of awards. The request contains nothing about you or your library.

**Crash reports and performance.** If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a diagnostic report to [Sentry](https://sentry.io/), the service the developer uses to track crashes. A report contains the app version, device model, iOS version, and technical details about the error. Some app sessions, chosen at random, also send performance timings. Reports never include your name, your library, or anything you typed, and they are used only to find and fix bugs.

**Anonymous usage counts.** To learn whether the app is working for people, it counts a few actions: opening the app, finishing first-time setup, saving your first rating, creating a list, and adding a title to your Watchlist. Each count is sent to [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com/), a privacy-focused analytics service, along with:

- the app and iOS version
- the device model, such as "iPhone 17" (the same for every phone of that model)
- the screen size and orientation
- how many times, and on how many different days, the app has been opened

A count never says which title, which list, what rating, or anything you typed.

Counts are linked to a random ID created when you install the app. That ID is not tied to you, your Apple Account, or your device's hardware identifiers, and TelemetryDeck scrambles it again when it arrives. To stop sending counts, turn off Settings → Share Anonymous Usage. That also erases the random ID and the open counts stored on your device, so if you turn it back on, you start fresh. See [TelemetryDeck's privacy policy](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/).

**Feedback you send.** When you use Send Feedback, your message goes to Sentry, where the developer reads it. You can add an email address if you'd like a reply. It is used only to answer you.

## How this information is used

To show movie and TV information, fix crashes and bugs, answer your feedback, and decide what to improve next (from the anonymous usage counts). None of it is used for advertising, and none of it is sold.

## Deleting your data

To remove your library, delete the app. To have feedback or an email address you sent deleted, email the address on the [support page](../support/) and it will be removed.

## Children

Silver Screen isn't made for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If a future version of the app adds accounts or sharing, the app will explain what changes before anything you saved leaves your device.
