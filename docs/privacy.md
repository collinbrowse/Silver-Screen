# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 15, 2026_

Silver Screen is built so your library stays yours. There is no account and no sign-up, no advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites. Nothing about you is sold or shared for marketing.

## What stays on your device

Your lists, ratings, notes, and TV progress are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. They are included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) like any other app data. The developer never receives them. Deleting the app deletes them.

## What leaves your device, and why

**Movie and TV information.** To show titles, people, search results, and streaming availability, the app requests them from [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/) (The Movie Database). Those requests include what you searched for or opened, but nothing from your library. TMDB receives your IP address as part of any internet request. See [TMDB's privacy policy](https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy).

**Trailers.** Trailers play from YouTube. When you play one, YouTube's [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies.

**Awards catalog.** The app checks GitHub for an updated awards list. That request contains nothing about you.

**Crash reports and performance.** If the app crashes or runs into an error, it sends a diagnostic report to [Sentry](https://sentry.io/), the developer's crash-reporting service. A report holds the app version, device model, iOS version, and technical details about the error. A sample of app sessions also sends performance timings. These reports don't include your name, your library, or an account, and they are used only to find and fix bugs.

**Feedback you send.** If you use Send Feedback, your message is sent to Sentry for the developer to read. You can add an email address if you'd like a reply; it is used only to answer you.

## Data used for

App functionality only: showing information, fixing crashes, and answering feedback. Nothing is used for advertising or sold.

## Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove your library. To have feedback or an email address you sent deleted, open a request through the [support page](../support/).

## Children

Silver Screen isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If a future version adds accounts or sharing, it will say what changes before anything you saved leaves your device.
